<h2 id="infobox1"><a href="#infobox1">Summary</a></h2>

<p>The Midnight Spectacular a variety show produced by Barrie Stead, director of the Nottingham Royal Centre, in the Opera House, Blackpool the previous week, raised over £14,000 for the Entertainment Artists' Benevolent Fund.</p>
<p>Performers including Cannon and Ball, Leslie Crowther and Lena Zavaroni gave their services free.</p>
<p>The show went on around midnight on Wednesday and finished at 5am on Thursday.</p>
