<h2 id="infobox5"><a href="#infobox5">Notes</a></h2>

<ul>
<li> A sum of £500 was presented to each of the acts that got a Merit and the Winner received  £1000, the challenge trophy and a contract for a summer season at Butlin's. The remaining acts each recived £100 all prizes were presented by Butlin's managing director Bobbie Butlin.</li>
<li class="red-bullet">Phil Jones would perform on Associated Television (ATV)'s <a class="external-link" href="https://newfacesatv.info/2022/01/15/22-jan-1978-series-six-20/#:~:text=Welsh%20comedian-,Phil%20Jones,-was%20selected%20to">New Faces</a> series 6 episode 20 on 22 January 1978.</li>
<li class="green-bullet">Best known for their marionettes, Paul & Peta Page also worked with <a class="external-link" href="https://genome.ch.bbc.co.uk/schedules/service_bbc_one_london/1977-07-09#at-17.30">The Hot Dogs</a>, an act of singing dog characters whose exact style (marionette, hand puppet, or ventriloquist dummy) is not currently known, credited on the 1977 BBC One children's series of the same name and performing the act in later variety shows.</li>
</ul>
