Lena Zavaroni stars in her own show at the New Webbington Country Club, Weston Super Mare.

<h2 id="infobox1"><a href="#infobox1">Glasgow Herald</a></h2>
<p>The newspaper reported on 10 January 1981 that Lena was suing the Hotel group that owned the venue (Cenmist Ltd. of London, trading as the New Webbington Country Club, Weston Super Mare), due to alleged non-payment of her £2850 fee.</p>
