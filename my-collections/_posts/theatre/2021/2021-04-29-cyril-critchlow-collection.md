<h2 id="infobox1"><a href="#infobox1">Materials Held For Lena Zavaroni As Part Of The Collection</a></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Vol 61 p 18 – 20:</strong> Five undated flyers (c.1975) for acts The Bachelors, Lena Zavaroni, The Grumbleweeds, Nolan Sisters, Charlie Williams, Ken Dodd, Kenny Ball, Norman Collier, Max Bygraves, Ken Goodwin, Moira Anderson, Beverley Sisters and The Vienna Boys Choir.</li>
<li><strong>Vol 62 p 37:</strong> Programme <a href="/1982-07-04-harold-fieldings-sunday-night-at-the-blackpool-opera-house">July 4th 1982</a>. Lena Zavaroni, Bernie Clifton, Roy Walker and Shep’s Banjo Boys.</li>
<li><strong>Vol 63 p 1 :</strong> Programme <a href="/1985-09-11-stars-midnight-spectacular">Sept 11th 1985</a> “Stars’ Midnight Spectacular”. Derek Batey, Norman Barrett, Jean Bennett, Black Lace, Canon and Ball, Leslie Crowther, Candlewick Green, Les Dennis and Dustin Gee, Johnny Casson, Dudley Doolittle, Billy Fontayne, Vince Hill, Terry King, Lovebirds, Damion Silver, Greg Rogers, Sinekos, Lena Zavaroni, Billy ‘Uke’ Scott, Ray Cornell and Gordon Scott.</li>
<li><strong>Vol 153 p 47:</strong> Photograph of Barbara Wilshere (Carol Broadbent), TV star from 1984 onwards. Signed fan club photograph of Lena Zavaroni, who appeared at the Winter Gardens Opera House in 1975, 1982 and 1985.</li>
</ul>
