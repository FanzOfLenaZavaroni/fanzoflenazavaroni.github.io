<p>The Playhouse Theatre is located at 126-130 High St, Weston-super-Mare BS23 1HP and is the same location as it was when Lena performed there.</p>
<p>The original Playhouse Theatre at the same location was gutted by a fire on 22 August 1964 so had to be rebuilt.</p>
