<h2 id="infobox1" class="infobox"><a href="#infobox1">Contract</a></h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Theatre Producer:</strong> <a href="/1916-12-04-harold-fielding">Harold Fielding</a>.</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Contract Signatory for Lena Zavaroni:</strong> Victor Zavaroni, director of Lena Zavaroni Public Limited Company (PLC).</p>
<p>It's interesting to note <strong>Victor Zavaroni</strong> signed contracts for Lena and not <strong>Dorothy Solomon</strong> and/or <strong>Phil Solomon</strong>, who were meant to be her management team and it shows that Victor was more involved in Lena's career than we may think.</p>
</li>
</ul>
