<h2 id="infobox1"><a href="#infobox1">Page Overview and Authorship</a></h2>
<p>This is a page from the comic emma written as if in the first person by <strong>Lena Zavaroni</strong> when more than likely it's based on questions she was asked by one of the comic's editors in a one of interview and then used across articles to create the idea she was writing a weekly article for the comic across a number of months.</p>
<p>The text uses "I" and "my" because <strong>Lena Zavaroni</strong> is being presented as having narrating her own personal experiences across multiple articles for various issues of the comic.</p>
<p>The top, left, and right margins of the entire page are framed by her name, with the phrase <strong>"it's LENA"</strong> printed repeatedly in a large, hollow outline font.</p>
<p>The page number <strong>22</strong> is centred at the bottom margin.</p>
<p>The date code <strong>EM. 19.8.78</strong> is printed at the bottom right corner.</p>

<h2 id="infobox2"><a href="#infobox2">Photographs</a></h2>
<p><strong>Top Right Photograph:</strong> A black-and-white square photo showing the singer <strong>Lulu</strong> and her hairdresser husband <strong>John Frieda</strong> smiling.</p>
<p>A white label in the lower right corner reads <strong>"Lulu and John."</strong></p>
<p><strong>Bottom Photograph:</strong> A wide, black-and-white horizontal rectangular photo centred across the lower half of the page.</p>
<p>It shows <strong>Lena Zavaroni</strong> looking downward on the left and her manager <strong>Dorothy Solomon</strong> smiling on the right.</p>
<p>A text label to the right reads, <strong>"A recent photo of Dorothy and I from my album."</strong></p>

<h2 id="infobox3"><a href="#infobox3">Text Arrangement</a></h2>
<p><strong>The Heading:</strong> The column begins at the top left with the cursive text, <strong>"Hello Again!"</strong></p>
<p><strong>The Body Text:</strong> The printed article text wraps around the left and bottom edges of the top right photo, sitting directly above the centered bottom photo.</p>
<p><strong>The Sign-off:</strong> The column ends with a cursive signature reading <strong>"Love from Lena"</strong> positioned directly above the right side of the bottom photo.</p>
