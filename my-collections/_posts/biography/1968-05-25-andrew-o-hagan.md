<h2 id="infobox1"><a href="#infobox1">Short Bio</a></h2>
<p><strong>Andrew O'Hagan</strong> (born 25 May 1968) is a Scottish novelist and non-fiction author. Three of his novels have been nominated for the Booker Prize and he has won several awards, including the Los Angeles Times Book Prize.</p>
