<h2 id="infobox1"><a href="#infobox1">English Translation</a></h2>

<p>The 10-Year-Old Millionaire</p>

<p>Lena Zavaroni</p>

<p>The young singer impersonates Rita Pavone and Alice Cooper on stage.</p>

<p>Wealth is a wonderful thing—especially when it arrives unexpectedly. Little Lena Zavaroni, from Scotland, is one such person; she is just ten years old and is already a millionaire.</p>

<p>Lena—described by music authorities as a "shooting star that burst onto the pop scene"—has a hit song, "Ma! He's Making Eyes at Me," that is still on everyone's lips. Her father, Victor Zavaroni, acts as her manager.</p>

<p>Lena Zavaroni, who has told the press that she forms a close-knit unit with her father Victor, her mother Hilda, and her younger sister Carla, is preparing for a major tour. The tour itinerary includes major European countries such as Germany, the Netherlands, Denmark, Italy, and France.</p>

<p>She shot to stardom rapidly.</p>

<p>Lena frequently appears on British BBC and German ZDF television broadcasts; she notes that she receives hundreds of letters from fans daily and has hired a special secretary to help answer them. Meanwhile, the young star does not neglect her schoolwork. She continues her studies with a private tutor to catch up on lessons that have occasionally fallen behind due to her busy schedule. "No" to film offers</p>

<p>Little Lena Zavaroni is currently turning down film offers due to her heavy workload. She openly admits that she draws inspiration from Rita Pavone's stage movements and imitates Alice Cooper's microphone-holding style.</p>
